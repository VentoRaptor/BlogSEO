MRT-3 Commuter Transcend Physical Form, Achieves Nirvana After Squeezing Into Overcrowded Bus at Shaw Boulevard

Is public transportation the secret to enlightenment and eternal happiness?

SHAW BOULEVARD — In a landmark triumph of spiritual endurance over mass transit pressures, 34-year-old IT professional Kyle Sinag reportedly shed his earthly form during Tuesday morning’s rush hour, achieving total enlightenment and a state of supreme cosmic oneness while wedged between a folding bicycle and a pillar of despair on a southbound EDSA carousel bus.


Eyewitnesses report that the transformation occurred precisely at 8:14 AM, just as the bus conductor screamed "Siksik pa, pwedeng-pwede pa!" and violently shoved the closing door against Sinag’s spine. Rather than being crushed into sub-atomic particles, Sinag’s mortal frame seemingly decompressed into pure, vibrating energy.


"At first, I thought he was just another commuter fainting from the humidity and the collective scent of every passenger's sweat and desperation," said commuter Brenda Gomez, who was pressed so tightly against Sinag that her left earring is now permanently embedded in his transcendent aura. "Then his physical body turned translucent, and instead of complaining about the air conditioning being broken, he started glowing with the serene, golden light of a thousand undisturbed Buddhas."


Transportation experts note that Sinag is the first Filipino commuter to successfully escape the earthly torment of Metro Manila traffic, and potentially even the absolute circus that is the Philippines itself via spontaneous spiritual ascension rather than a filing for resignation.


"I have found La Peace" says the Sinag himself.
